# G3 findings — the harness sees what it is meant to see

Date: 2026-09-10
Engine: llama.cpp `llama-server` build `b10839-0cae43063`, Windows, CUDA, RTX 4060 Laptop (8 GB)
Records: [`g3/`](g3/) (pilot JSON lines; no local paths). Regenerate the verdicts with `python scripts/g3_check.py --dir docs/findings/g3`.
Pre-registration: `docs/preregistration/2026-09-10-analysis-plan.md`, section 11.2

**Verdict: gate G3 passed on all three models.** Every arm was proven, every
snapped restore was bit-exact, and the hybrid's natural-boundary restores
diverged. The main study can run once the analysis code is committed and tested
(pre-registration 11.3).

## What ran

`scripts/run_study.py --pilot` on each model, using the pre-registered server
flags plus `--slot-save-path`. The pilot is 20 turns per model: the short band
of `generate_study_turns(10, seed=20260911, …)` in both strata, trace hash
`f94004397c8cecc2…` (matches the pre-registration). Each turn ran the six
pre-registered arms: recompute, restore at the natural boundary and restore at
the snapped boundary, in two replicates. State-byte comparison was on.

## Result

| Model | Turns | Valid arms | Snapped restore bit-exact vs recompute | Natural-boundary drift (positive control) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ornith-1.5-9B (hybrid) | 20 | 120 / 120 | 40 / 40 (20 turns × 2 replicates) | **20 / 20 turns** (required) | G3_PASS |
| Qwen3-4B (dense) | 20 | 120 / 120 | 40 / 40 | not required | G3_PASS |
| Spark-X2.5-4B (sliding window) | 20 | 120 / 120 | 40 / 40 | not required | G3_PASS |

- **Arm proofs.** Every restore evaluated only its suffix, every saved slot held
  exactly `k` tokens, and every recompute evaluated the whole prompt. No arm
  errored.
- **Mitigation, on chat-templated agent traffic.** Snapping the reuse boundary
  down to a multiple of 512 made every restore bit-exact: identical tokens and
  bitwise-identical top-10 logprobs at every generated position, on all three
  architectures. G1 showed this on one raw-text prompt per model; this is the
  first evidence on chat-templated agent traffic, and it is pilot data.
- **Floor.** The two recompute replicates were bit-exact on every turn of every
  model (reported, not gated). The zero-floor branch of the pre-registered tests
  is therefore the one expected in the main study.

## Disclosure

The pre-registration says that only the G3 checks are looked at in the pilot.
The ad hoc summary used while writing this note also printed the count of
natural-boundary turns with nonzero drift for **Qwen3 and Spark (20 / 20 each)**,
which G3 does not require. That is pilot data, excluded from the confirmatory
analysis, and consistent with G1, where the dense model's drift at misaligned
boundaries was small but nonzero. No action changes, exact-match rates, drift
magnitudes or state-byte results were computed or looked at for any model.
`scripts/g3_check.py` prints drift counts only where the positive control
applies.
